About Us

NOMADTIPS3 is an independent football-analysis prototype built around disciplined manual selection, transparent records and practical live-match statistics.

What we do

We review raw football information such as recent form, home and away performance, standings, goals for and against, direct meetings, common opponents, team availability, fatigue and motivation.

Manual-first approach

Selections are made through the NOMAD SYSTEM / Manual Set 2 process. The project does not copy public prediction articles or automatically generate core picks from another website.

Transparent presentation

Locked picks, numeric odds, confidence, predicted score and supplementary markets are presented consistently across Today’s Picks, Statistics, Share Poster and Live Test.

Live match tools

Where data coverage is available, Live Test displays scores, events, match statistics and an attack-momentum graph. Live availability can vary by competition and data coverage.

What NOMADTIPS3 is not

NOMADTIPS3 does not accept wagers, deposits or withdrawals; does not hold customer funds; and does not operate a gambling account or betting service. It is an informational football-analysis and statistics project.
