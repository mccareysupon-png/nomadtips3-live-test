How nomadtips3 works

Use this page to understand live statuses, locked signal details, statistics and Line alerts membership.

Live statuses

WatchingThe match is being monitored while live evidence develops.
CloseThe match is near the signal conditions, but nothing is locked yet.
SignalConditions are met. The selected team, Asian Handicap, odds and detection time are recorded.

How to read a signal

Example locked signalTeam A +1 @ 1.64
68′Entry 0–1
Team A is the selected team.
+1 is the locked Asian Handicap.
1.64 is the locked market price.
68′ · 0–1 is the match time and score at detection.

Using the Live page

Open Live to see current monitored matches.
Follow Watching → Close → Signal as the match situation changes.
Tap View live evidence to expand the match details.
When a Signal appears, read the selected team, AH, locked odds, entry score and time.

Reading Statistics

Win — successful settled signal.
Loss — unsuccessful settled signal.
Push — neutral settled result.
Avg odds — average locked price in the record.

Statistics are built from recorded locked signals so past results remain visible after settlement.
