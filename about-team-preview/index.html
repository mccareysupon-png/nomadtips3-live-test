How the roles fit together

MJ owns the direction of the product and decides what the platform should make easier to understand. K challenges the football interpretation and analytical logic. The separation is deliberate: product decisions and analytical conclusions should not be treated as the same job.

Why this layout works

The people appear after the main About statement, not before it. This keeps NOMADTIPS3 itself as the primary subject while still giving the project a recognizable human face.

Transparent records

Recorded selections and outcomes should remain reviewable, including unsuccessful outcomes and legitimate corrections.

Visible context

Scores, match events, attacking activity, market references and model outputs are presented as evidence to inspect rather than guarantees.
