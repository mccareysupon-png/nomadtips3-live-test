Information may be used to operate and secure the website, diagnose technical problems, improve reliability, maintain test features and respond to enquiries.

The website may be hosted and delivered using third-party infrastructure such as GitHub Pages. Service providers may process limited technical information under their own privacy and security practices to provide the service.

NOMADTIPS3 does not sell personal information. Information may be disclosed only when reasonably necessary to operate the service, protect security, comply with applicable requirements or respond to a lawful request.

8. Retention and choices

Information is retained only as reasonably necessary for operation, security and correspondence. You can clear local browser data through your browser settings and may contact us about information you previously sent by email.

9. Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to manualprototype@nomadtips3.com.

This policy may be updated when NOMADTIPS3 adds accounts, analytics, subscriptions, payment services or other features that process additional information.
