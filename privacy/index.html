NOMADTIPS3 may use essential browser storage such as localStorage where required to preserve interface settings, temporary application state, locally retained match information or other technical functionality. NOMADTIPS3 does not sell personal information. Information may be disclosed where reasonably necessary to operate or secure the service, to work with service providers, to investigate abuse, to protect legitimate rights, or to comply with an applicable legal requirement or lawful request.
