Privacy Policy

This policy explains the limited information that may be processed when you use nomadtips3, including technical storage, contact details and optional subscription services.

Effective: 19 August 2026Applies to nomadtips3 web services

1. Our approach

nomadtips3 is a football prediction and live match-information platform for entertainment purposes. We aim to process only the information needed to operate the website, preserve user-selected settings where applicable, deliver optional alerts and respond to contact requests.

We do not sell personal information. nomadtips3 does not operate a betting account, hold betting balances or collect wagering information.

2. Information that may be processed

Website and device information

Basic technical information may be processed automatically by the browser, hosting infrastructure or security services when a page is requested. This can include request time, browser type, device information, network information and diagnostic logs needed to keep the service available and secure.

Browser storage

The website may use local storage, session storage or similar browser features for operational functions such as keeping temporary display state, remembering previously viewed live information or retaining settings on the device.

Contact information

If you contact us, we may process the email address and message content you choose to provide so we can respond.

Optional Line alerts membership

If you use the optional alerts service, limited account or pairing information may be processed to connect your messaging account with membership status and deliver locked signal notifications.

3. Payments and third-party services

Subscription checkout is handled by an external payment provider. nomadtips3 does not store your full payment card number or card security code. The payment provider may process payment, billing and transaction information under its own privacy terms.

Messaging delivery, payment processing and hosting infrastructure may involve third-party service providers. Those providers process information according to their own policies and the services they supply.

4. How information is used

  • Operate, maintain and secure the website.
  • Display live football information, recorded signals and historical statistics.
  • Maintain optional membership and alert-delivery functions.
  • Diagnose technical problems and improve reliability.
  • Respond to support or contact requests.
  • Prevent misuse or disruption of the service.

5. Cookies and similar storage

nomadtips3 may use essential browser storage or technical cookies where necessary for site operation, security, performance or user experience. We do not describe such storage as advertising cookies unless an advertising feature is actually introduced.

You can clear browser storage through your browser settings. Some site functions may need to recreate necessary storage after it is cleared.

6. Retention and security

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably needed for the purpose for which it is processed, operational record-keeping, service security or support. Historical football signal records are part of the public transparency function and may remain available as performance history.

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures for the service, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Your choices

You may choose not to use optional membership or messaging features. You may also clear browser storage on your device. If you have a privacy question or want to ask about information you provided directly to us, contact us using the address below.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the service, technical features or data-handling practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when a material revision is published.
