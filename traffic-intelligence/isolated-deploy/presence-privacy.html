Consented visitor measurement at BALL46

This notice covers Online browsers, Unique browsers and Sessions in BALL46 VISITOR MONITOR. The operator is BALL46 Team. Contact jay@ball46.com.

  • Measurement starts only after you choose Accept. Declining does not affect your use of the website. These measurements are not used for advertising or tracking across websites.
  • Measurement is limited to approved public pages on ball46.com and www.ball46.com. Member, sign-in and payment pages are excluded.
  • A random browser ID expires after 30 days. A session ID changes after 30 minutes of inactivity. IDs are stored in each host's LocalStorage. We do not fingerprint your device.
  • The browser sends only random IDs, an approved public page path and consent. It does not send query strings, URL fragments, referrers, typed text or click details. Activity signals such as scrolling or key presses only update a local inactivity timer; their details are never sent.
  • BALL46's Cloudflare server adds the receipt time, country and Mobile/Desktop device category. Random IDs are hashed before storage in the separate VISITOR MONITOR KV namespace. Each measurement record expires within 26 hours of its most recent update.
  • This feature does not store full or masked IP addresses in its database. The application does not log visitor payloads. Cloudflare processes IP addresses and network information to deliver and protect the service.
  • A heartbeat is sent about every 45 seconds while the page is visible and there has been activity within 30 minutes. Online means a consenting browser with a heartbeat within two minutes; it does not prove a person is looking at the screen. KV updates can take 60 seconds or longer to appear.
  • Unique browsers are not unique people. Changing browser, device or host, or clearing LocalStorage, may create a new ID. Sessions counts session IDs active in the last 24 hours. No historical visitor records are invented.
  • Both Accept and Decline / withdraw close the banner immediately. To change your choice, use Privacy settings in the website footer. Withdrawal stops heartbeats, removes browser IDs and sends a request to delete that ID's measurements. KV deletion may take time to propagate. If the deletion request fails, server records still expire within 26 hours. Your declined choice remains in the browser so the banner does not ask again.
  • Do Not Track (DNT) and Global Privacy Control (GPC) disable this measurement. If browser storage is unavailable, measurement stays off.
  • Reports are available only to the owner through Cloudflare Access. The public Collector accepts writes but has no report-reading API. IP samples shown in the owner dashboard come from Cloudflare Request Logs, a separate source; browser IDs are not linked to these IP samples.

Cloudflare provides the processing infrastructure. Storage and processing may take place across its global network under the Cloudflare privacy policy. Contact BALL46 Team with questions about your data or rights.
